MITT

Multimodal Interface for Teleoperation and Tracking. Say what you want, and a real tank goes and gets it.

This is a report of recorded runs, not a live tank. The robot needs its hardware, so nothing here drives anything.

How a mission runs

Words become a target. The camera frame and the target go to Meta SAM 3.1. The tank turns toward what SAM finds, drives, checks that it has arrived, and moves on to the next target. If it loses the target, it backs up and looks, sweeps the neck, then turns the chassis. If nothing turns up, it stops.

Known limits

  • Motion is open loop. Short spins undershoot, and timing drifts with battery charge.
  • Arrival is judged from the image, and some paths can report ARRIVED too early.
  • With two cats in the room, it hunts whichever one it sees first.
  • The parser's safety comes from a word list, so new wording could still get through. The Pi's phone page still has the older, looser parser.
  • The arm is manual only.
